The Platform
What is BodyMetrix?
BodyMetrix is a clinical-grade body composition platform that combines an FDA Class I cleared ultrasound device with a required subscription. The BX2K measures adipose tissue and lean mass directly at the tissue level — unaffected by hydration, meal timing, exercise intensity, or time of day. The subscription platform connects the device to a complete body composition management system for assessment, client record management, longitudinal tracking, and result reporting. The device operates through the platform. They are not sold or used separately.
Get BodyMetrix
Is BodyMetrix a standalone device?
No. The BX2K and the subscription platform are one integrated system. The device requires an active subscription to operate. Both must be present in your cart before checkout completes.
Get BodyMetrix
Who is BodyMetrix built for?
BodyMetrix is built for professionals — not consumers. It serves elite fitness and personal training, wellness and nutrition, sports medicine, GLP-1 and weight loss programs, youth sports compliance, and professional and collegiate sports. Not sure which tier fits your practice?

Is BodyMetrix FDA cleared?
Yes. The BodyMetrix BX2K is FDA Class I cleared as a general wellness and body composition measurement device. It supports and documents clinical decision-making — it does not make clinical diagnoses or replace physician judgment.

BodyMetrix BX2K And What It Measures
How accurate is BodyMetrix?
BodyMetrix has been validated in 50+ peer-reviewed clinical studies across athletic populations, clinical settings, weight management programs, and youth sports compliance frameworks. Results are unaffected by hydration, meal timing, caffeine, or exercise — variables that significantly compromise bioimpedance analysis.
See The Evidence
How long does an assessment take?
A standard 3-site protocol completes in approximately 90 seconds. The 7-site protocol completes in under 5 minutes.
Does BodyMetrix require specialist training to operate?
No. BodyMetrix is operator-independent — any trained provider produces a consistent, defensible assessment regardless of who runs it. All subscribers have access to the BodyMetrix Support Center for onboarding guides, assessment references, and best practice content.
Get BodyMetrix
What software does BodyMetrix use?
BodyMetrix currently operates on BodyView — PC and Mac compatible. The Provider tier uses BodyViewProFit. The Youth Sports Compliance tier uses BodyViewWR. Software and firmware updates are available for download on demand — updates are not automatic.
See The Evidence
Does ultrasound gel come with the device?
Yes. One 2oz ultrasound gel bottle is included with every BX2K as a starter supply. Ultrasound gel is required for every scan. Replenishment is available directly through BodyMetrix — one-time at $29.99 per 3pk or monthly subscription at $19.99 per 3pk with flexible quantity selection.
See The Evidence
Can I use other gels besides BodyMetrix Ultrasound Gel?
No. Using other mediums may cause damage to the device and void your warranty. Only BodyMetrix Ultrasound Gel is approved for use with BodyMetrix devices.
See The Evidence
Warranty And Protection
What is the Enhanced Lifetime Warranty?
The Enhanced Lifetime Warranty provides new device replacement coverage for the full device lifecycle — available exclusively to active subscribers. Coverage is tied directly to your active subscription. If your subscription lapses, coverage terminates immediately and permanently and does not reinstate. Keep your subscription active and renew before your renewal date.

What happens if my subscription lapses?
If a renewal payment fails, BodyMetrix retries payment automatically for 10 days. After that a lapse is declared — your device remains active during a grace period but suspension follows if payment is not received. Enhanced Lifetime Warranty coverage terminates permanently after a lapse exceeding 100 days. Full lapse and reinstatement terms are governed by the BodyMetrix Master EULA.

I run a GLP-1 or weight loss program. Is BodyMetrix right for me?
Yes. BodyMetrix measures fat mass and lean mass independently — unaffected by the fluid balance shifts that make BIA unreliable during active GLP-1 therapy. Every visit generates a documented, longitudinal body composition record that confirms fat loss without muscle sacrifice and justifies therapy duration with objective data.

I own a gym or personal training practice. How does BodyMetrix help my business?
BodyMetrix shows clients exactly what is changing — fat decreasing, muscle growing — in a 90-second assessment that happens in your facility every visit. Clients who see real numbers stay invested. Providers have generated tens of thousands of dollars in additional revenue directly tied to BodyMetrix scanning as a service.

I work in sports medicine. What does BodyMetrix do for my practice?
BodyMetrix brings a single operator-independent body composition standard into the sports medicine practice — accurate enough for return-to-play documentation, portable enough for any clinical or field setting, and defensible from intake through discharge without referrals or data gaps.
